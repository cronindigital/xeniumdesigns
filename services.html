Our Services

What we build

Every business has different data in different places. We build dashboards that bring it all together.

Analytics Dashboards

Your website is generating data every day. We pull it from Google Analytics, Matomo, Plausible, or any analytics platform and turn it into a dashboard you actually enjoy looking at.

  • Daily, weekly, and monthly traffic views
  • Traffic sources and channel breakdown
  • Top pages and user behaviour
  • Bounce rates, session duration, conversions
  • Custom date ranges and comparisons

Sales & Revenue Dashboards

Know exactly how your business is performing. We connect to your payment platform and show revenue, transactions, and trends in real time.

  • Daily revenue and transaction tracking
  • Revenue by product, service, or category
  • Refund and chargeback monitoring
  • Month-over-month and year-over-year comparisons
  • Currency conversion for international sales

Email & Marketing Dashboards

Stop logging into three different email tools to check your stats. We aggregate your email marketing data into one clean view.

  • Campaign open rates and click-through rates
  • Subscriber growth and churn
  • Series performance averages
  • Monthly campaign summaries
  • A/B test results at a glance

Multi-Source Dashboards

The real power. Combine analytics, sales, and email data into a single view that shows the complete picture of your business.

  • All your key metrics in one place
  • Cross-platform insights (e.g. traffic vs sales)
  • Unified date ranges across all sources
  • Export-ready data tables
  • Embeddable in your existing website
