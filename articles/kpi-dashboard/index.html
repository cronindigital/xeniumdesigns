KPI Dashboards

KPI Dashboard Resources

Real-world KPI dashboard examples across industries, see what metrics successful businesses track and how they display them.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) are the numbers that tell you whether your business is on track. A KPI dashboard puts those numbers in one view so you can monitor progress toward your goals every day, not just at the end of the quarter.

The guide below shows ten real-world KPI dashboard examples across different industries, so you can see what works, what metrics matter, and how to design a dashboard that fits your business.
