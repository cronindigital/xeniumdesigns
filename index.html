What is a business intelligence dashboard?

A business intelligence dashboard is a custom-built visual display of your key business data. It pulls information from multiple platforms (analytics, sales, email, social media) into one clean view so you can see how your business is performing at a glance; without logging into five different tools.

How much does a custom dashboard cost?

Our custom dashboard development service is £997 for the initial build, plus £200 per month for ongoing support, monitoring, and one data update per month. There are no hidden fees. Compare that to hiring a data analyst at £35,000+ per year or an agency build at £10,000+.

How long does it take to build a dashboard?

Most custom dashboards are built and live within 2 weeks. The process starts with a discovery call to understand your data sources and goals, then we build, deliver, and walk you through everything.

What data sources can you connect to?

We connect to Google Analytics, Matomo, Shopify, Stripe, Teachable, MailerLite, Mailchimp, ConvertKit, and any platform with an API. If your data is accessible, we can pull it into your dashboard.

Do I need technical skills to use the dashboard?

Not at all. Your dashboard works like a website; open it in any browser on your phone, tablet, or laptop. We handle all the technical setup and automated data collection. You just check your numbers.
